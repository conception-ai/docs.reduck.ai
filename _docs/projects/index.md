A **project** is a private workspace for scripts you build yourself, as opposed to the
[official library](/docs/official-library) that Reduck's team maintains.

Projects let you automate flows specific to your own stack and share them with your team without
exposing them to the broader Reduck community.

## Setting one up

1. **Create a project** at [reduck.ai/projects/create](https://reduck.ai/projects/create).
2. **Assign scripts to it** — either ask your agent to do so while it is writing the script, or
   move an existing script into the project afterwards and set its visibility to team-only.
